7. Read a garage conversation

Two words are new. Read for the general meaning without translating every word.

Garage: Good morning. West Street Garage.

Owner: My car is not reliable. The engine sometimes does not start.

Garage: Do you have an appointment?

Owner: No, I do not. Is a mechanic available today?

Garage: Yes, he is. He repairs electric cars.

Owner: Is the garage busy?

Garage: Yes, it is. The mechanic is available at three.

Owner: Is the car ready at five?

Garage: Yes, it is.

Choose the best summary

  1. A mechanic calls an owner about a new car.
  2. An owner calls a garage because the car has a problem.
  3. An owner makes an appointment for the weekend.

Answer from the conversation

  1. Is the owner's car reliable?
  2. Does the owner have an appointment?
  3. Is a mechanic available?
  4. Does the mechanic repair electric cars?
  5. Is the garage busy?
  6. Is the car ready at five?
Answers

Best summary: 2.

1. No, it is not.   2. No, the owner does not.

3. Yes, he is.   4. Yes, he does.

5. Yes, it is.   6. Yes, it is.
