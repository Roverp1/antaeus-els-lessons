Lesson 5 · Beginner · 60 minutes

Subjects and negative sentences

Подлежащее и отрицательные предложения

Today's goal: find the whole subject and make positive and negative sentences with to be and action verbs.

Цель: находить подлежащее целиком и строить утвердительные и отрицательные предложения.

1. Homework check

Read your homework aloud. For each sentence:

  1. Read the complete sentence.
  2. Mark the whole subject and the verb.
  3. Say whether it uses to be or an action verb.
  4. Correct the sentence if needed, then read it again.

What is the whole subject? Which pronoun replaces it? Description or action? One person or more than one?

2. Rule 1: find the whole subject

Rule card 1

Replace the whole subject

my brother he
the car it
the engine it
your parents they
the tires they

Replace the whole subject, not its first word.

Work through one example

[Your parents] [work] [every day].

Your parents means more than one person. Replace it with they: They work every day.

Explain the rule

Why does your parents become they, not you?

Hide the card and rebuild the row

Complete subject Pronoun Example

Focused practice

Mark the complete subject and verb. Then replace the subject with a pronoun.

  1. My brother drives to work every morning.
  2. Your parents work every day.
  3. The tires are new.
  4. The car needs a tire.
  5. The engine is powerful.
Check after you finish

[My brother] [drives] to work every morning. He drives to work every morning.

[Your parents] [work] every day. They work every day.

[The tires] [are] new. They are new.

[The car] [needs] a tire. It needs a tire.

[The engine] [is] powerful. It is powerful.

3. The complete map

Now look at the full system. Fill in the handwritten table one row at a time during the lesson.

Verb type Subject Positive Negative
to be I I am a driver. I am not a mechanic.
to be he/she/it/one person or thing My brother is an engineer. My brother is not a mechanic.
to be you/we/they/more than one The tires are new. The tires are not old.
action verb I / you / we / they They work every day. They do not work every day.
action verb he/she/it/one person or thing My brother works every day. My brother does not work every day.

4. Rule 2: negative action verbs

Rule card 2

Subject Negative form Example
I / you / we / they do not + basic verb I do not drive every day.
he / she / it
one person or thing		 does not + basic verb My brother does not drive every day.

Does not already have the -s. The action verb has no -s.

does not drive   does not drives

Work through one example

Positive: My brother drives to work every morning.

Negative: My brother does not drive to work every morning.

My brother becomes he, so use does not. Then change drives back to drive.

Explain the rule

Why do we say does not drive, not does not drives?

Hide the card and rebuild the row

Subject Negative form Example
one person or thing

Choose

  1. I (do not / does not) drive every day.
  2. My brother (do not / does not) drive every day.
  3. Your parents (do not / does not) work every day.
  4. The car (do not / does not) need a tire.

Make the sentence negative

  1. I drive to work every morning.
  2. My brother works every day.
  3. Your parents practice math.
  4. The engine starts every morning.
Answers

I do not drive to work every morning.

My brother does not work every day.

Your parents do not practice math.

The engine does not start every morning.

the car / not need / a tire

5. Rule 3: choose the verb type

Rule card 3

Meaning Use Example
identity or description am not / is not / are not My car is not new.
action do not / does not + basic verb My car does not start every morning.

First decide: description or action?

Choose

  1. My car (is not / does not) new.
  2. My car (is not / does not) start every morning.
  3. Your parents (are not / do not) mechanics.
  4. Your parents (are not / do not) work every day.
  5. The tires (are not / do not) cheap.
  6. The car (is not / does not) need a tire.
Answers

1. is not   2. does not   3. are not   4. do not   5. are not   6. does not

6. Read and understand

My brother is an engineer. He works every day. He is a driver. He drives an old car. The car is comfortable, but it does not start every morning. My brother fixes the car.

  1. Listen while the tutor reads.
  2. Mark the subject and verb in each sentence.
  3. Read the text aloud.
  4. Write true or false. Correct each false sentence.
  1. My brother is a mechanic.
  2. He works every day.
  3. The car is old.
  4. The car starts every morning.
  5. My brother fixes the car.
Answers

1. False: My brother is an engineer.   2. True   3. True

4. False: The car does not start every morning.   5. True

7. Speak and write

Speak

Say four true sentences:

  1. one positive sentence with to be
  2. one negative sentence with to be
  3. one positive sentence with an action verb
  4. one negative sentence with an action verb

Write

Complete one set:

I am . My car is .
I am not . My car is not .
I every day. My car every morning.
I do not every day. My car does not every morning.

8. Exit check no notes

Write one complete sentence for each prompt.

  1. your parents / work / every day positive
  2. my brother / not drive / every morning negative
  3. the tires / not new negative
  4. the engine / start / every morning positive
  5. the car / not need / a tire negative
Answers

Your parents work every day.

My brother does not drive every morning.

The tires are not new.

The engine starts every morning.

The car does not need a tire.

Homework

  1. Correct any mistakes from today's exit check.
  2. Write two positive and two negative sentences about yourself.
  3. Write two positive and two negative sentences about your car or your brother.

Use both to be and action verbs. Use only words from your vocabulary list.
