Check what is in the car

Today's goal: ask about lost things and tell a coworker what is missing.

3. What if something is missing?

A customer asks about something left in a car. You need to ask whether it is there and tell the customer if it is missing.

Present: There is a scarf in the Honda.

Absent: There is no scarf in the Honda.

Ask: Is there a scarf in the Honda?

No scarf означает, что шарфа нет. В вопросе is стоит перед there.

Open the check-and-report reference. Keep it available for every new form today, including the closing practice.

4. Report what is absent

NounBuildExample
singular: scarfThere is no + singular noun + place.There is no scarf in the Honda.
plural: ringsThere are no + plural noun + place.There are no rings in the Honda.

With no, do not add a/an or a number.

No scarf is singular, so use is. No rings is plural, so use are, even though the number present is zero.

6. Answer from the garage record

A short answer uses there, not it or they. The full negative answer uses not: No, there is not. / No, there are not. Keep the reference open.

Honda: a jacket on the back seat; an earring under the front seat; no scarf; no gloves.

Office: two rings and three buttons.

Ask the full question, then give a short answer. For a no answer, also report what is absent.

  1. one / jacket / on the back seat
  2. one / scarf / in the Honda
  3. two / ring / in the office
  4. two / glove / in the Honda
  5. one / earring / under the front seat
  6. three / button / in the office
Answers

1. Is there a jacket on the back seat? Yes, there is.

2. Is there a scarf in the Honda? No, there is not. There is no scarf in the Honda.

3. Are there two rings in the office? Yes, there are.

4. Are there two gloves in the Honda? No, there are not. There are no gloves in the Honda.

5. Is there an earring under the front seat? Yes, there is.

6. Are there three buttons in the office? Yes, there are.

7. Read a conversation

Read to find out what Lena is looking for and where it is.

Lena: Hi Mia. Is there a scarf in my Honda?

Mia: No, there is not. There is no scarf in the car, but your scarf is in the office.

Lena: Are there two rings in the office?

Mia: Yes, there are. The rings are in a bag.

Lena: Is the Honda ready?

Mia: Yes, it is. You can pick it up tomorrow.

8. Choose the sentence job

Keep both the there reference and the sentence builder available. First decide whether you are asking about a thing, locating a known item, or talking about an action. Then make the sentence.

  1. A customer lost a ring. Ask if one is in the Honda.
  2. You know which ring the customer means. Ask if that ring is in the Honda.
  3. Garage record: no scarf in the Honda. Tell the customer what is missing.
  4. You know where the customer's scarf is: in the office. Tell the customer.
  5. Mia checks each car before it leaves. Report her routine.
  6. The owner may have an appointment. Ask about it.
Answers

1. Is there a ring in the Honda?

2. Is the ring in the Honda?

3. There is no scarf in the Honda.

4. The scarf is in the office.

5. Mia checks each car before it leaves.

6. Does the owner have an appointment?
