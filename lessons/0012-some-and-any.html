3. Some and any: talk about amounts

When you check what is in the garage, you need to say what is there, ask about it, or say what is missing. You do not always need an exact amount or number.

Some: say what is there

Use some in a positive statement when something is there, but you do not give its exact amount or number.

There are three tools in the bag. We give the exact number.

There are some tools in the bag. There are tools, but we do not say how many.

There is some oil in the bottle. There is oil, but we do not say how much.

Some: какое-то количество, не обязательно маленькое. Точное число или количество сейчас не важно.

Any: ask if something is there

You need oil, but you do not know if there is oil in the garage. In this kind of question, use any.

Is there any oil in the garage?

Есть ли в гараже масло? Спрашиваем, есть ли масло, без точного количества. Здесь any не означает «каждый».

Not any: say what is missing

If there is no oil, you can say this with not any.

There is not any oil in the garage.

В гараже нет масла. Not any означает, что масла нет совсем.

You already know another way to say this:

There is no oil. = There is not any oil.

There are no tools. = There are not any tools.

Отрицание создаёт not. Само any не означает «нет».

Choose is or are from the noun

Some and any do not change the is/are rule. Look at the noun after them.

Plural countable noun → are. Tools is plural.

There are some tools. Are there any tools?

Uncountable noun → is. Oil is uncountable here.

There is some oil. Is there any oil?

The same choice applies in negatives: There are not any tools. There is not any oil.

Tools: plural countable noun → are. Oil: uncountable noun → is. Some и any этого не меняют.

For one new item, keep a/an + singular noun: There is a tool. In today's amount patterns, some and any go before plural countable nouns or uncountable nouns.

8. Why two buckets?

Read once for the general idea. Then read again and choose A, B, or C. Ask about any words you need help with.

Word help

bucket = ведро; sponge = губка; rinse = ополаскивать водой.

for rinsing your sponge = для ополаскивания губки; a small part of the car = небольшой участок автомобиля; put it back = положить её обратно; often = часто.

To wash your car, you need two buckets. Put water and car soap in one bucket. The other bucket is for rinsing your sponge. Use the sponge with soap to wash a small part of the car. Then rinse it in the water bucket before you put it back in the soap. Do this often as you wash the car.

Adapted from steps 1, 4, and 5 of Brett & Kate McKay's car-washing guide.

  1. What is the advice about?
    1. Buying a car for work
    2. Washing a car by hand
    3. Fixing a car at home
  2. What is the other bucket for?
    1. Checking the engine oil
    2. Washing the car seats
    3. Rinsing out your sponge
  3. What do you do just before putting the sponge back in the soap?
    1. Rinse the sponge first
    2. Repair the engine first
    3. Wash the seats first

In Then rinse it, point to what it means.

Answers

1. B   2. C   3. A

It means the sponge.
