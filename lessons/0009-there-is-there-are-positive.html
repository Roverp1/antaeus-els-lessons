Lesson 11 · Beginner · There is / there are

What is in the car?

Что находится в машине?

Today's goal: report one thing or several things in a place.

Цель: сообщить, что один или несколько предметов находятся в определённом месте.

1. Check the homework

Recover the plural ending

Complete the two vocabulary answers as parts of their sentences.

  1. You usually wear one on each hand: ______. (glove)
  2. A jacket often has several at the front: ______. (button)
Answers

1. gloves   2. buttons

Make the message natural

Original: Hello I think I left my black wallet in the car Black Honda. Do you can check it? I can pick it up tomorrow afternoon.

Natural version: Hello. I think I left my black wallet in a black Honda. Can you check it? I can pick it up tomorrow afternoon.

The original message communicates the important information. Keep Can you check it? as one useful phrase. Its complete grammar comes later.

4. A new communicative job

Present simple describes a known subject: The mechanic is busy. Today we need a different opening to report what is present in a place.

There is a jacket on the back seat.

There are two gloves on the floor.

Здесь there is / there are сообщает, что кто-то или что-то есть или находится в этом месте.

Check the situation

  1. How many jackets are on the back seat?
  2. How many gloves are on the floor?
Answers

1. One jacket.   2. Two gloves.

5. Notice both number signals

Meaning Opening Noun phrase Place
one There is a/an + singular noun on the back seat
more than one There are number + plural noun on the floor

Number controls two places.

one → is and a singular noun

more than one → are and a plural noun

6. Build the rule card

Open the positive there is / there are reference.

DecisionBuildExample
one There is + a/an + singular noun + place. There is an earring under the seat.
more than one There are + number + plural noun + place. There are two gloves on the floor.

Close the reference. Reconstruct both rows aloud, then reopen it and check. Use the full forms there is and there are today.

There are three buttons under the jacket. Mia calls the car owner. He needs the jacket and gloves, but he does not need the earring or buttons.
