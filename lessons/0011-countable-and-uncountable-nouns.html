Lesson 13 · Beginner · Countable and uncountable nouns

Things and amounts

Перчатки можно назвать поштучно. С маслом, водой и деньгами нужна другая строка.

Today's goal: choose a noun phrase for separate items or an amount, then use the right sentence form.

Цель: различать countable и uncountable nouns и правильно использовать их в знакомых предложениях.

1. Check the homework

Read one negative report and one question with its short answer from the last homework. Then compare these two questions.

Is there a scarf in the office? Ask whether a scarf is present.

Is the scarf in the office? Ask about the particular scarf you both mean.

Оба вопроса правильные. Во втором мы уже говорим о конкретном шарфе.

In a one-word gap such as There is ______ handbag in the car, write only no.

2. Brief check of earlier grammar

Close the references for this check of earlier lessons. Say what the task asks you to do, then give one complete sentence. Item 3 needs only a short answer.

Example: the car / not ready negative description

Answer: The car is not ready.

Сначала объясните задание своими словами. В пунктах 1, 2, 4, 5, 6 нужно полное предложение; в пункте 3 только короткий ответ.

  1. no scarf / in the Honda Report what is absent.
  2. two / glove / on the back seat Ask if they are present.
  3. Record: a jacket is on the back seat. Is there a jacket on the back seat? Answer yes.
  4. the mechanics / not busy Make a negative description.
  5. the owner / have an appointment Make a question.
  6. the scarf / in the office Ask about the known scarf's location.

4. Why the noun rule needs another row

There are two gloves on the seat. Gloves are separate items.

There is oil on the floor. Here, oil names a liquid.

To report what is there or what someone needs, we must know whether the noun names separate items or an amount. In English, these uses are called countable and uncountable.

NounMeaning hereNatural example
waterвода как жидкостьThere is water on the floor.
oilмасло для двигателяThe engine needs oil.
moneyденьги, сумма денегThe owner needs money for a new engine.

Можно измерить количество воды. Но здесь мы не говорим two waters или a water. Money тоже uncountable в English, хотя по-русски «деньги».

5. Choose the noun phrase

First choose the kind

Say C for countable or U for uncountable. Example: ring → C.

Назовите C или U. Полное предложение пока не нужно.

  1. glove
  2. water as a liquid
  3. engine
  4. money
Answers

1. C   2. U   3. C   4. U

Now choose the phrase

Say the correct noun phrase. Example: one car → a car.

Выберите и произнесите группу существительного.

  1. one engine: a engine / an engine
  2. engine oil as a liquid: oil / an oil
  3. two gloves: two glove / two gloves
  4. water as a liquid: a water / water
Answers

1. an engine   2. oil   3. two gloves   4. water

6. Put the noun into the sentence

Say is or are for each gap. Example: There ______ a car outside → is.

Назовите только пропущенное слово.

  1. There ______ oil on the floor.
  2. There ______ two gloves on the seat.
  3. There ______ no water on the floor.
  4. The money ______ for a new engine.
Answers

1. is   2. are   3. is   4. is

Say four complete sentences

Use the references. Example: no oil / on the floor; report absence → There is no oil on the floor.

Теперь произнесите полное предложение.

  1. oil / on the floor Report what is present.
  2. two / glove / in the Honda Ask if they are present.
  3. no water / on the floor Report what is absent.
  4. the owner / need money Make a question.
Answers

1. There is oil on the floor.

2. Are there two gloves in the Honda?

3. There is no water on the floor.

4. Does the owner need money?

8. Read a personal account

Read the account. Choose A, B, or C for each question.

For ten years, I took my car to a garage for oil changes. I usually sat and watched the mechanics work. I wanted to do the job myself. It looked easy, and I did not like paying someone else to do it. But I did nothing about it for years. I was too lazy to try. Then I finally learned how to change the oil in my car. It felt good to do it myself.

Adapted from Brett & Kate McKay's account.

  1. Why did the writer want to learn?
    1. To do the job himself
    2. To sell his car soon
    3. To work at another garage
  2. Why did he wait so long?
    1. He had no money for it.
    2. He was too lazy to try.
    3. He had no car to use.
  3. Which happened last?
    1. He took his car to a garage.
    2. He watched the mechanics at the garage.
    3. He learned to change his car's oil.
Answers

1. A   2. B   3. C
