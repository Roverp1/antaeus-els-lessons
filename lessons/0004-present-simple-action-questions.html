Questions with action verbs

Вопросы с глаголами действия

Today's goal: recover the grammar you know, then ask yes/no questions about routines and facts.

Цель: повторить знакомую грамматику и задавать общие вопросы о привычках и фактах.

1. Homework check

Read up to six homework sentences aloud.

  1. Read the whole sentence.
  2. Mark the complete subject and the verb.
  3. Say whether it uses to be or an action verb.
  4. Correct the sentence if needed, then read it again.

2. Check what you remember

Do this without notes. Spelling and punctuation are not the target.

Replace the subject

  1. your parents → ______
  2. the car → ______
  3. Mark the complete subject: My brother drives to work.

Make the sentence negative

  1. The tires are new.
  2. My brother drives to work.
  3. They work at home.
Answers

1. they   2. it   3. my brother

The tires are not new.

My brother does not drive to work.

They do not work at home.

3. Recover the two grammar lanes

Meaning Positive Negative
who, what, or how it is
to be		 am / is / are am not / is not / are not
what someone or something does
action verb		 verb / verb-s do not / does not + basic verb

Choose the grammar lane

Say to be or action verb. Do not build the sentence yet.

  1. the car / new
  2. the car / need a tire
  3. my brother / engineer
  4. my brother / drive to work
  5. the tires / expensive
  6. the engine / work

Build the negative sentences

  1. the car / not new
  2. the car / not need a tire
  3. your parents / not mechanics
  4. your parents / not drive to work
Answers

The car is not new.

The car does not need a tire.

Your parents are not mechanics.

Your parents do not drive to work.

Tutor gate

Continue to questions if at least three sentences are correct and the student can explain one grammar lane. Otherwise, repeat this section with the map visible and use Sections 10–11 for familiar oral practice.

4. Why we need questions

Positive and negative sentences give information. Questions ask whether the information is true.

You drive to work.

You do not drive to work.

Do you drive to work?

My brother works at home.

My brother does not work at home.

Does my brother work at home?

5. Present simple question rule

Write this down

Subject Question form Example
I / you / we / they
more than one		 Do + subject + basic verb? Do your parents work at home?
he / she / it
one person or thing		 Does + subject + basic verb? Does your brother drive to work?

Does has the -s. The action verb has no -s.

Does your brother drive?   Does your brother drives?

The complete subject stays together:

Does [your brother] drive to work?

Do [your parents] work at home?

Work through one example

your brother / drive to work?

  1. Complete subject: your brother
  2. Pronoun: he
  3. Question word: does
  4. Basic verb: drive
  5. Question: Does your brother drive to work?

Explain the rule

Why do we say drive, not drives, after does?

8. Two kinds of questions

First decide: description or action?

To beAction verb
Is your brother an engineer? Does your brother work at home?
Are your parents mechanics? Do your parents drive to work?

Choose the first word

  1. (Is / Does) your car new?
  2. (Is / Does) your car need a tire?
  3. (Are / Do) your parents mechanics?
  4. (Are / Do) your parents drive to work?
Answers

1. Is   2. Does   3. Are   4. Do

10. Read the dialogue

A: Do you drive to work?

B: No. I work at home.

A: Does your brother work at home?

B: No. He drives to work.

  1. Mark the complete subject in each question.
  2. Circle do and does.
  3. Underline the basic action verb.
  4. Read both roles aloud.
