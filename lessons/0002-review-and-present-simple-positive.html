3. What are tenses?

A tense helps us say when a situation or action happens. English has many tense forms. Today we only need the basic time map.

Past

Before now

I worked yesterday.

Present

Now, usual life, facts

I work every day.

Future

After now

I will work tomorrow.

Сегодня мы не учим Past или Future. Карта нужна, чтобы увидеть место Present Simple.

4. Why present simple?

Present simple is a present-tense form. We use it to talk about routines, habits, facts, and schedules. It is useful when you describe your work, your car, or what happens regularly.

Use Example
routine рутинаI drive to work every day.
habit привычкаMy brother drinks coffee in the morning.
fact фактA car needs fuel.
schedule расписаниеThe class starts at six.

Two different jobs for verbs

My car is fast. Описание: какой автомобиль?

My brother drives a Subaru. Действие: что делает брат?

Use is for a description. Use drives for an action. Do not mix them: My brother is drives a Subaru.

5. Present simple: positive sentences

Action verbs tell us what a person or thing does: drive, work, need, repair, like. In a positive present-simple sentence, the verb changes only with he / she / it and one person or thing.

Write this down

Subject Verb Example
I / You / We / TheydriveWe drive to work.
He / She / ItdrivesHe drives a Subaru.
one person or thingdrivesMy brother drives a Subaru.

Rule: he / she / it / one person or thing + verb-s.

Пока учим только правило -s. Другие написания будут позже.

Choose the correct verb

  1. My brother (drive / drives) a Subaru.
  2. I (repair / repairs) my bike.
  3. The engine (need / needs) oil.
  4. We (work / works) every day.
  5. My friend (like / likes) sports cars.
Check after you choose

1. drives   2. repair   3. needs   4. work   5. likes
