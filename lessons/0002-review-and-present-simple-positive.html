3. What are tenses?

A tense shows when a situation or action happens. English has many tense forms. Today we only need this simple time map.

Past

Before now

I worked yesterday.

Present

Now, usual life, facts

I work every day.

Future

After now

I will work tomorrow.

Сегодня мы не учим Past или Future. Карта показывает, где находится Present Simple.

4. Why present simple?

Present simple is a present-tense form. Use it for routines, habits, facts, and schedules. It helps you talk about work, cars, and things that happen regularly.

Use Example
routine рутина I drive to work every day.
habit привычка I study math every day.
fact факт My car needs a tire.
schedule расписание I start work at six.

5. To be or an action verb?

To be is a verb, but it does not say what someone does. It says who someone is or what something is like. Action verbs say what someone or something does.

Write this down

What do you want to say? Use Example
who someone is кто это am / is / are I am a driver.
what something is like какой am / is / are Math is hard.
what someone does что делает an action verb I drive to work every morning.
what something does что делает an action verb The engine starts.

In a positive present-simple sentence, do not put am / is / are before an action verb.

I am drive to work every morning.
I drive to work every morning.

Remember this for later

Later, action verbs use do / does in questions and negatives. To be uses its own forms: am / is / are. You do not need to use do / does today.

6. Present simple: positive sentences

Action verbs say what a person or thing does: drive, work, need, practice, fix. In a positive present-simple sentence, add -s only after he / she / it or one person or thing.

Write this down

Subject Verb Example
I / You / We / They drive We drive to work.
He / She / It drives He drives to work.
one person or thing drives My brother drives to work.

Rule: he / she / it / one person or thing + verb-s.

Пока учим только правило -s. Другие написания будут позже.
