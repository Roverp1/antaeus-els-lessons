3. Why we use short answers

In conversation, we usually answer a yes/no question with more than only yes or no. The short answer repeats the grammar word from the question.

A: Does your brother work in a garage?

B: Yes, he does.

A: Is he a mechanic?

B: No, he is not. He is an engineer.

4. Action-verb short answers

Rule card

Question begins with Yes No
Do I/you/we/they...? Yes, I/you/we/they do. No, I/you/we/they do not.
Does he/she/it...? Yes, he/she/it does. No, he/she/it does not.

Repeat do or does. Do not repeat the action verb.

Yes, he does.   Yes, he works.

Work through one example

Does the car need oil?

  1. The complete subject is the car.
  2. Replace it with it.
  3. The question begins with Does.
  4. Answer: Yes, it does. / No, it does not.

Explain the rule

Why do we answer Yes, it does, not Yes, it needs?

5. Answer action-verb questions

Give a true short answer. Use the complete subject to choose the pronoun.

  1. Do you work in an office?
  2. Does your brother drive to work?
  3. Do your parents wash their car on the weekend?
  4. Does your car need a new tire?

Choose the correct short answer

  1. Does the mechanic work in a garage?
    (Yes, he does. / Yes, he is.)
  2. Do the customers need a reliable car?
    (Yes, they do. / Yes, they are.)
  3. Does the engine work?
    (No, it does not. / No, it is not.)
  4. Do your parents work at home?
    (No, they do not. / No, they are not.)
Answers

1. Yes, he does.   2. Yes, they do.

3. No, it does not.   4. No, they do not.

6. Two kinds of questions and answers

Grammar type Question Short answer
to be Is the car reliable? Yes, it is.
action verb Does the car need oil? Yes, it does.
to be Are your parents engineers? No, they are not.
action verb Do your parents work at home? No, they do not.

7. Read a garage conversation

The conversation has a few new words. Read for the general meaning. Do not stop to translate every word.

Garage: Good morning, West Street Garage.

Owner: Hi. Is a mechanic available this afternoon?

Garage: Yes, he is. Does your car start?

Owner: Yes, it does, but the engine does not work well.

Garage: Does the car need oil?

Owner: I do not know.

Garage: That is fine. Do you need an appointment today?

Owner: Yes, I do.

Garage: The mechanic is available at four.

Choose the best summary

  1. A mechanic calls a customer about a new car.
  2. A car owner calls a garage because the engine has a problem.
  3. A customer works in a garage every afternoon.

Answer from the conversation

  1. Is a mechanic available?
  2. Does the car start?
  3. Does the owner know if the car needs oil?
  4. Does the owner need an appointment?
Answers

Best summary: 2.

1. Yes, he is.   2. Yes, it does.

3. No, the owner does not.   4. Yes, the owner does.

Natural spoken negatives

Read these aloud. The meaning is the same.

No, it is not. No, it isn't.
No, it does not. No, it doesn't.
No, they do not. No, they don't.

Use full forms in the grammar exercises. Recognize contractions in conversation.

8. Ask, answer, and report

Ask the tutor six true questions. Use both grammar types.

  1. Two questions about the tutor
  2. Two questions about the tutor's family
  3. Two questions about a car, work, or the weekend

For each question:

  1. Choose to be or an action verb.
  2. Build and ask the complete question.
  3. Listen to the short answer.
  4. Report the information as a complete sentence.

Student: Do you work at home?

Tutor: Yes, I do.

Student reports: You work at home.
