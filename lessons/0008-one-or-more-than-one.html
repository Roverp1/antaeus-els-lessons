4. Read for the situation

This is a natural text. Some grammar is new. Do not analyze or copy the new forms; read to understand what happens.

Mia repairs cars at West Street Garage. Near closing time, she checks a customer's car. There is a jacket on the back seat, and there are two gloves on the floor. She also finds an earring and a loose button under the driver's seat. Mia puts the things in the office and calls the owner. She tells him about the jacket, gloves, earring, and button.

Choose the meaning

  1. What is the text mainly about?
    1. A customer collects a repaired car from the garage.
    2. A mechanic finds several things in a customer's car.
    3. An owner calls the garage about a missing jacket.
  2. Where are the things at first?
    1. Inside the office
    2. Inside the car
    3. Outside the garage
  3. Who does Mia call?
    1. The car owner
    2. A garage mechanic
    3. A new customer
Answers

1. B   2. B   3. A

Choose a or an by the next sound.

a before a consonant sound: a jacket, a mechanic

an before a vowel sound: an earring, an owner
