Step 4 of 7 · Choose the verb job

What do you want to say?

To be says who someone is or what something is like. An action verb says what someone or something does.

Step 6 of 7 · Recall

Say the rule without looking

For one person or thing, what happens to an action verb in a positive present-simple sentence?

Say your answer aloud. Then reveal the rule.

He / she / it, or one person or thing: add -s.
My brother drives to work. The car needs a tire.

Step 7 of 7 · Independent check

Do this without notes

Choose the correct word, then say why.

  1. My brother (is / are) an engineer.
  2. The tires (need / needs) air.
  3. Your parents (work / works) every day.
  4. The car (is / need) fast.
Check answers after you finish

1. is: my brother = he. 2. need: the tires = they. 3. work: your parents = they. 4. is: fast is a description.
