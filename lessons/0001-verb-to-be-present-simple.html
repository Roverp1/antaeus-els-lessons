Lesson 3 · Beginner · Present simple · ~40 min

The verb to be in present simple

Глагол «to be» в настоящем простом времени

Today's goal: say what things or people are, and ask about them.

Цель урока: говорить, кем/чем являются люди и вещи, и спрашивать об этом.

By the end you can: make positive, negative, and question sentences with am / is / are; never drop the subject again.

Why this lesson matters

Look at your homework from last time:

I get out of bed.

In the morning, I drink coffee.

And then I go to the car.

Go to work.

Then I come home, wash up and go to sleep.

Most of this is correct English. The line in red is wrong — but not because of the present simple. It's wrong because the subject is missing. In Russian you can drop it:

«Иду на работу» — по-русски можно без «я».

In English you cannot. The subject must be there, every time:

Go to work.

I go to work.

Today we fix this. The verb to be is the cleanest place to start, because you cannot drop the subject with it. You cannot say "Am a driver" — it sounds broken immediately. Every sentence forces you to put the subject first.

1. Why we need the verb "to be"

Most verbs are actions: drive, work, fix, break. But English also needs a verb when there is no action — when you only say who someone is, what something is like, where, or where from. For that, English uses to be.

1 · Write this down

"to be" links a subject to:

  • identity — кто это — I am a driver.
  • description — какой — The car is fast.
  • location — где — He is at work.
  • origin — откуда — She is from Russia.

Russian lets you drop the verb here. English does not.

Russian (ok)English wrongEnglish right
Я водитель. I driver. I am a driver.
Машина быстрая. The car fast. The car is fast.
Он на работе. He at work. He is at work.

Without "to be" the English sentence has no verb — and an English sentence needs a verb, always.

2. Subject pronouns

1 · Write this down

The seven subject pronouns in English:

Pronoun Russian Used for
I я yourself
You ты / вы the person you speak to (one or many)
He он a man / a boy
She она a woman / a girl
It он, она, оно (неодуш.) a thing, an animal, an idea
We мы you + others
They они other people / things (plural)

Russian has «он / она / оно» for objects too (стол — он, машина — она). English uses it for all things. A car is always it, not "he" or "she".

3. The verb "to be" — three forms

3 · Write this down

"to be" has three present forms:

I  →  am    You / We / They  →  are    He / She / It  →  is

Subject to be Example
I am I am a driver.
You are You are a mechanic.
He is He is an engineer.
She is She is from Russia.
It is It is a fast car.
We are We are drivers.
They are They are new tires.

Note: the subject is always there, always first. English never drops it. "Am a driver" is not a sentence.

Adjectives — прилагательные

  • cheap — дешёвый — It is cheap.

  • powerful — мощный — It is powerful.

5. Positive, negative, question

4 · Write this down

Positive — утвердительная

I am a driver.
He / She / It is fast.
You / We / They are drivers.

Negative — отрицательная (add "not")

I am not a driver. (I'm not)
He / She / It is not fast. (isn't)
You / We / They are not drivers. (aren't)

Question — вопросительная

Swap order: verb + subject.
Am I a driver?   Is he fast?   Are they drivers? Меняем порядок: глагол первым, подлежащее вторым.

Short answers — краткие ответы

Question Yes No
Am I ...? Yes, I am. No, I 'm not.
Is he ...? Yes, he is. No, he isn't.
Are they ...? Yes, they are. No, they aren't.

Contractions: I'm, you're, he's, she's, it's, we're, they're. Negative short forms: isn't, aren't. Note: "amn't" doesn't exist — say "I'm not".

D · Free production

Write 5 sentences about a car — yours, your family's, or one you like. Use to be in every sentence. Don't drop the subject.

Example: My car is a Toyota. It is red. It is not new, but it is comfortable. The engine is powerful. We are happy with it.
