3. Choose the grammar lane

Do not build the sentence immediately. First find the word that carries the main meaning after the subject.

Rule card 1

After the subject Use Example
a person, thing, description, or place BE The car is reliable.
an action verb: work, need, use, wash, study ACTION VERB The car needs a tire.

If you already have an action verb, do not add BE.

My brother works in an office.
My brother is works in an office.

Work through two examples

the car / reliable

Reliable describes the car. There is no action verb, so use BE: The car is reliable.

the car / need a tire

Need is the action verb, so do not add BE: The car needs a tire.

Explain the rule

Why does The car is reliable use BE, but The car needs a tire does not?

4. One person or thing changes the action verb

Rule card 2

Verb ending Change Examples
most verbs add -s work → works
need → needs
drive → drives
-ch, -ss, -sh, -x, -zz add -es wash → washes
fix → fixes
consonant + y change y to i, add -es study → studies
three useful forms remember them have → has
go → goes
do → does

Use these forms only with he, she, it, or one person or thing.

Work through one example

My brother / wash / his car on the weekend.

  1. Complete subject: my brother
  2. Replace it with: he
  3. Wash ends in -sh.
  4. Add -es: washes.

My brother washes his car on the weekend.

6. Positive, negative, and question

The ending moves to does in negatives and questions.

Positive Negative Question
He washes his car. He does not wash his car. Does he wash his car?
She studies math. She does not study math. Does she study math?

Choose the correct verb

  1. My brother (fix / fixes) cars.
  2. My brother does not (fix / fixes) cars.
  3. Does my brother (fix / fixes) cars?
  4. She (study / studies) math every day.
  5. She does not (study / studies) on the weekend.
  6. Does she (study / studies) at home?
Answers

1. fixes   2. fix   3. fix

4. studies   5. study   6. study

7. Read for the general meaning

The text has a few new words. Do not stop at every new word. Read the whole text and understand the main situation.

Alex is a mechanic. He works in a small garage near his home. He usually fixes cars for the same customers. He checks each car carefully because he wants it to be safe and reliable. On the weekend, Alex does not work in the garage. He washes his own car and spends time at home.

  1. Listen while the tutor reads.
  2. Read the text silently. Do not translate every word.
  3. Read the text aloud.
  4. Choose the best summary.
  1. Alex sells new cars.
  2. Alex fixes cars during the week and rests on the weekend.
  3. Alex works at home every day.

True or false?

  1. Alex is a mechanic.
  2. He works in an office.
  3. He wants the cars to be safe.
  4. He works in the garage on the weekend.
  5. He washes his own car.
Answers

Best summary: 2.

1. True   2. False   3. True   4. False   5. True
