Words from the conversation

Word Meaning Natural example
appointment запись, назначенная встреча I have an appointment at the garage at four.
available свободный; доступный The mechanic is available this afternoon.
owner владелец The car owner calls the garage.
problem проблема, неисправность The engine has a small problem.
service техническое обслуживание The car needs a service.
busy занятый The garage is busy in the morning.

Choose the word

Use appointment, available, owner, problem, service, busy.

  1. The person who has the car is its ______.
  2. The mechanic is free at four. He is ______ at four.
  3. I call the garage and make an ______ for Friday.
  4. The engine does not work well. It has a ______.
  5. The mechanic checks the car during a ______.
  6. The garage has many customers today. It is ______.
Answers

1. owner   2. available   3. appointment

4. problem   5. service   6. busy

Level 4: build and answer

Build the complete question, then write the short answer shown.

  1. the mechanic / careful? yes
  2. the mechanic / check every car? yes
  3. the engine / reliable? no
  4. the engine / need oil? yes
  5. the customers / in the garage? no
  6. the customers / have an appointment? yes
Answers

Is the mechanic careful? Yes, he is.

Does the mechanic check every car? Yes, he does.

Is the engine reliable? No, it is not.

Does the engine need oil? Yes, it does.

Are the customers in the garage? No, they are not.

Do the customers have an appointment? Yes, they do.

Level 6: read and answer

Nina owns an old car. The car is comfortable, but it is not reliable. The engine sometimes does not start, so Nina calls a busy garage. A mechanic is available at ten, and Nina makes an appointment for a service. The mechanic checks the engine and fixes the problem. The car does not need a new engine.

Answer with short answers.

  1. Is Nina a car owner?
  2. Is her car reliable?
  3. Does the engine always start?
  4. Is a mechanic available?
  5. Does Nina make an appointment?
  6. Is the garage busy?
  7. Does the car have a problem?
  8. Does the car need a new engine?
Answers

1. Yes, she is.   2. No, it is not.

3. No, it does not.   4. Yes, he is.

5. Yes, she does.   6. Yes, it is.

7. Yes, it does.   8. No, it does not.
