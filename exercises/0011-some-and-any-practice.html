Word help

bucket = ведро; sponge = губка; rinse = ополаскивать водой.

Car soap means soap for washing a car. It is uncountable here.

Session 1

Level 1: some or any?

Write one word. In this exercise, use some in positive statements and any in questions about what is available and in negative statements. Example: There are ______ tools. → some.

В каждом пункте запишите только some или any.

  1. There is ______ soap in this bottle.
  2. Are there ______ gloves in the box?
  3. There are not ______ batteries at the garage.
  4. Is there ______ money for a service?
  5. There are ______ sponges in the bag.
  6. There is not ______ gasoline for the car.
Sample answers

1. some   3. any

2. any   4. any   5. some   6. any

Level 2: build the noun phrase

For one new item, use a/an. Otherwise use some and the correct noun form. Keep the adjective. Write only the noun phrase.

Example: one / new / car → a new car

Example: more than one / glove → some gloves

Запишите только noun phrase. More than one = больше одного; an amount = какое-то количество.

  1. one / old / engine
  2. more than one / new / tool
  3. an amount / oil
  4. one / sponge
  5. more than one / battery
  6. an amount / equipment
Sample answers

1. an old engine   3. some oil

2. some new tools   4. a sponge   5. some batteries   6. some equipment

Level 3: build full sentences with there

Use some in positive statements, any in these questions about what is available, and not any in negative statements. Use plural noun forms when the task asks about more than one item.

Example: water / in the bottle; negative statement → There is not any water in the bottle.

Запишите полное предложение. В пункте 5 добавьте короткий ответ.

  1. oil / in the garage Make a positive statement.
  2. glove / in the bag Ask a neutral question about available gloves.
  3. appointment / this afternoon Make a negative statement about appointments.
  4. equipment / in the office Make a positive statement.
  5. soap / in the bottle Ask a neutral question. Answer no.
Sample answers

1. There is some oil in the garage.

5. Is there any soap in the bottle? No, there is not.

2. Are there any gloves in the bag?

3. There are not any appointments this afternoon.

4. There is some equipment in the office.

Session 2

Level 4: choose the sentence job and build

Write one full sentence for each task. Use the sentence builder when needed.

Example: the car / ready; question about this known car → Is the car ready?

Сначала определите смысл задания, затем постройте полное предложение.

  1. bucket / in the garage Make a positive statement about several buckets with some.
  2. the equipment / not ready Make a negative description.
  3. the owner / need new tires Make a question.
  4. water / in the bucket Ask a neutral question with any.
  5. the mechanics / not use this tool Make a negative statement.
  6. money / for a new engine Make a negative statement with there and not any.
Sample answers

2. The equipment is not ready.

4. Is there any water in the bucket?

1. There are some buckets in the garage.

3. Does the owner need new tires?

5. The mechanics do not use this tool.

6. There is not any money for a new engine.

Level 5: read for meaning

Read the passage. Write A, B, or C for each question.

Reading help

from the top down = сверху вниз; a small part at a time = по одному небольшому участку; often = часто.

keep the car wet = смачивайте машину водой, чтобы она оставалась мокрой; prevent water and soap spots = не дать появиться пятнам от воды и мыла; a gentle stream of water = несильная струя воды.

Wash the car from the top down. Work on a small part of the car at a time, and rinse the sponge often. Keep the car wet while you wash it. This helps prevent water and soap spots. At the end, rinse the car from the top down again. Use a gentle stream of water.

Adapted from steps 5 and 6 of Brett & Kate McKay's car-washing guide.

  1. What is the advice about?
    1. Buying a car for work
    2. Repairing a car at home
    3. Washing a car in stages
  2. In the text, where do you start washing?
    1. At the very top
    2. At the very bottom
    3. Inside the car first
  3. Why do you keep the car wet?
    1. To keep soap in the bucket
    2. To prevent water and soap spots
    3. To fix problems with the engine

For question 3, underline the sentence that helped you choose.

Sample answers

1. C

2. A   3. B

Evidence for 3: This helps prevent water and soap spots. Underlining this together with the preceding sentence is also valid.

Session 3

Level 6: repair one mistake

Each sentence has one grammar mistake. Rewrite the full sentence without changing the intended meaning. Keep the not any pattern where it appears.

Example: There is some tools in the bag. → There are some tools in the bag.

В каждом предложении одна ошибка. Сохраните смысл и перепишите предложение целиком.

  1. There are some equipment in the garage.
  2. Are there any sponge in the box?
  3. There is not some soap in the bottle.
  4. Does the mechanic needs oil?
  5. The two buckets is in the office.
Sample answers

1. There is some equipment in the garage.

4. Does the mechanic need oil?

2. Are there any sponges in the box?

3. There is not any soap in the bottle.

5. The two buckets are in the office.

Level 7: the word's job in this sentence

Write noun, adjective, or verb for the bold word. Example: The driver is ready. → noun.

Запишите noun, adjective или verb для выделенного слова. Всё предложение переписывать не нужно.

  1. The oil is expensive.
  2. Mia needs two sponges.
  3. The equipment is ready.
  4. There are two buckets in the office.
  5. The mechanic rinses the sponge.
  6. There are some tools in the box.
Sample answers

2. verb   4. noun

1. adjective   3. verb   5. verb   6. noun

Level 8: tell Leo about a garage

Leo asks about a garage he might visit. Choose a real garage you know, or imagine one. Write him a message of 4-6 sentences. Describe the place and say whether you like it. Choose the facts and wording yourself.

Напишите Leo о настоящем или придуманном гараже. Опишите его и скажите, нравится ли он вам. Вы сами выбираете детали и предложения.

Sample answers

Possible opening: Hi Leo,

One fictional example: Hi Leo, I like this garage. It is small, but the mechanics are helpful. The equipment is new. The owner works here every morning. My brother uses this garage too.

Accept different facts and wording. Check whether Leo can understand the description and opinion. Give a few corrections on familiar grammar. Some/any is required only in the controlled levels, not in this message.

Optional extra

Do this only if you studied Section 7 of Lesson 14. Use the extra reference row and sentence builder.

Write one complete sentence. Example: the owner / have / some tools; positive → The owner has some tools.

  6. the drivers / need / any water Make a neutral question.
