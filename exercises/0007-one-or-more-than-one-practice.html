Level 5: understand a natural message

Some grammar in this message has not been taught. Read for the situation; you do not need to explain the unfamiliar forms.

Lena calls West Street Garage because she cannot find one of her earrings. She sometimes travels in her friend's car, and that car is at the garage for a service. Mia checks the car and then the office. She sees an earring beside a jacket in the office. Lena comes at lunchtime, thanks Mia, and takes it home.

Choose the meaning

  1. Why does the woman call?
    1. She needs a car service.
    2. She cannot find an earring.
    3. She wants a new jacket.
  2. Where is the earring?
    1. In the garage office
    2. Under the customer's car
    3. Inside the woman's home
  3. What happens at the end?
    1. Mia keeps the earring.
    2. The car owner collects it.
    3. Lena takes the earring home.
  4. Why is the friend's car at the garage?
    1. It needs a service.
    2. Its owner works there.
    3. Lena leaves it there.
  5. When does Lena come to the garage?
    1. Near closing time
    2. Early in the morning
    3. At lunchtime
