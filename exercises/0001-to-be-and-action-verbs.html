To be and action verbs

Упражнения: to be и глаголы действия

1. Sort the sentences

Write to be or action verb.

  1. Math is hard.
  2. I study math every day.
  3. I am a driver.
  4. I drive to work every morning.
  5. Addition is easy.
  6. We practice addition.
  7. My brother is an engineer.
  8. My brother works every day.
Answers

1. to be   2. action verb   3. to be   4. action verb

5. to be   6. action verb   7. to be   8. action verb

2. Choose the correct word

  1. Math (is / study) hard.
  2. I (am / study) math every day.
  3. My brother (is / works) an engineer.
  4. My brother (is / drives) to work every morning.
  5. The engine (is / starts) powerful.
  6. My brother (is / starts) work at six.
  7. The car (is / needs) a new tire.
  8. We (are / practice) addition every day.
Answers

1. is   2. study   3. is   4. drives

5. is   6. starts   7. needs   8. practice

  4. We ______ math every day.
  5. The engine ______ powerful.
  6. My brother ______ work at six.
  7. My brother ______ an engineer.
  8. My brother ______ every day.
Answers

1. am   2. drive   3. is   4. study

5. is   6. starts   7. is   8. works
