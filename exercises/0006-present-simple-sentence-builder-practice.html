Choose the cell and build

Выбираем ячейку и строим предложение

Keep the sentence builder open. Mark the form, choose the block and row, then build from one cell.

Отметьте форму, выберите блок и строку, затем используйте одну ячейку.

Practise new and recent words

Use ready, repair, appointment, available, owner, problem, service, busy.

  1. The mechanic is free at three. He is ______ at three.
  2. The car is finished. It is ______.
  3. The mechanic fixes the engine. He ______ it.
  4. I call the garage and make an ______.
  5. The person who has the car is the ______.
  6. The engine does not work well. It has a ______.
  7. The mechanic checks the car during a ______.
  8. The garage has many customers. It is ______.
Sample answers

1. available   2. ready   3. repair

4. appointment   5. owner   6. problem

7. service   8. busy

Level 1: choose the route

Write: block → pronoun row → form column. Do not build the sentence.

  1. the owner / not busy negative
  2. the owner / repair cars question
  3. the customers / available positive
  4. the customers / not have appointments negative
  5. the engine / not reliable negative
  6. your parents / work in an office question
Sample answers

1. to be → he/she → negative

2. action verb → he/she → question

3. to be → they → positive

4. action verb → they → negative

5. to be → it → negative

6. action verb → they → question

Level 2: build from the cell

Mark the form, say the route, and write the complete sentence.

  1. the mechanic / ready positive
  2. the mechanic / repair the car question
  3. the garage / not busy negative
  4. the owner / have an appointment positive
  5. the customers / not available negative

2. Does the mechanic repair the car?

Level 5: read and answer

Leo is the owner of a small garage. The garage is busy every day. Leo repairs cars and checks engines. A customer calls. Her car does not start. Leo is available at two. The customer makes an appointment for a service. Her car has a small problem. It does not need a new engine. Leo repairs the car. It is ready at five.

Answer with a complete short answer.

  1. Is Leo a garage owner?
  2. Is the garage busy?
  3. Does Leo work in an office?
  4. Does the customer have an appointment?
  5. Does the car need a new engine?
  6. Is the car ready at five?
Sample answers

1. Yes, he is.

2. Yes, it is.

3. No, he does not.

4. Yes, she does.

5. No, it does not.

6. Yes, it is.

Level 6: write

Write six connected sentences about the garage in Level 5.

Use both to be and action verbs. Mark the route before each sentence.

Sample answers
