Level 1: choose the opening

Write there is or there are. The noun phrases are already complete.

  1. ______ a mechanic in the office.
  2. ______ two cars at the garage.
  3. ______ an engine outside the garage.
  4. ______ three problems with the car.
  5. ______ a button under the seat.
  6. ______ four gloves in the office.
  7. ______ an appointment at five.
  8. ______ two owners at the garage.
Sample answers

1. There is   2. There are   3. There is

4. There are   5. There is   6. There are

7. There is   8. There are

Level 2: complete both number signals

  5. There ______ an owner at the garage.

Level 5: understand a garage report

Mia checks a customer's car after a service. There is a black jacket in the office. There are two gloves on the back seat. There is an earring under the front seat. There are four buttons on the floor. Mia checks the objects and writes a report for the garage owner.

Choose the meaning

  1. Where is the jacket?
    1. Inside the garage office
    2. Under the front seat
    3. On the car floor
  2. Choose the number of gloves.
    1. One glove
    2. Two gloves
    3. Three gloves
  3. Where is the earring?
    1. Inside the garage office
    2. Under the front seat
    3. On the car floor
  4. Choose the number of buttons.
    1. Two buttons
    2. Three buttons
    3. Four buttons
  5. What is the text mainly about?
    1. Things in one car
    2. People in one office
    3. Services at one garage
Sample answers

1. A   2. B

3. B   4. C   5. A
