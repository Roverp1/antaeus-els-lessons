Check and report

Спрашиваем о вещах и сообщаем, чего нет

Keep the check-and-report reference open. This homework follows Lesson 12. Use it for new negative statements, questions, and short answers. The sentence builder can help with earlier grammar in the mixed levels.

Новую конструкцию тренируйте с открытой памяткой. После ответа проверьте образцы.

Level 1: choose the negative form

Complete each absence report. The noun after no shows whether to use is or are. For item 3, write the word that means zero.

  1. There ______ no scarf on the back seat.
  2. There ______ no rings in the office.
  3. There is ______ handbag in the car. (none is there)
  4. There ______ no gloves on the floor.
  5. There ______ no necklace under the jacket.
  6. There ______ no buttons in the Honda.
Sample answers

1. is   2. are

3. no   4. are   5. is   6. are

Level 2: report missing things

Write full sentences from the garage record. Keep the singular or plural noun shown.

  1. no earring / on the front seat
  2. no gloves / under the jacket
  3. no scarf / in the office
  4. no rings / in the Honda
  5. no button / on the floor
  6. no necklaces / under the seat
Sample answers

1. There is no earring on the front seat.

2. There are no gloves under the jacket.

3. There is no scarf in the office.

4. There are no rings in the Honda.

5. There is no button on the floor.

6. There are no necklaces under the seat.

Level 3: ask about the inventory

Turn each positive report into a question. Put is or are first.

  1. There is a jacket on the back seat.
  2. There are two gloves on the floor.
  3. There is a handbag in the office.
  4. There are three rings under the seat.
  5. There is an earring under the jacket.
  6. There are four buttons in the car.
Sample answers

1. Is there a jacket on the back seat?

2. Are there two gloves on the floor?

3. Is there a handbag in the office?

4. Are there three rings under the seat?

5. Is there an earring under the jacket?

6. Are there four buttons in the car?

Level 4: ask and answer from one record

Read the complete record. Build each question and answer it. After a no answer, also report what is absent.

Honda: a jacket on the back seat; a scarf under the front seat; no handbag; no gloves.

Office: two rings; no buttons.

  1. one / jacket / on the back seat
  2. one / handbag / in the Honda
  3. two / ring / in the office
  4. three / button / in the office
  5. one / scarf / under the front seat
  6. two / glove / in the Honda
Sample answers

1. Is there a jacket on the back seat? Yes, there is.

2. Is there a handbag in the Honda? No, there is not. There is no handbag in the Honda.

3. Are there two rings in the office? Yes, there are.

4. Are there three buttons in the office? No, there are not. There are no buttons in the office.

5. Is there a scarf under the front seat? Yes, there is.

6. Are there two gloves in the Honda? No, there are not. There are no gloves in the Honda.

Level 5: choose the right sentence system

Read the situation before building the sentence. Do you need there is/are, to be with a known subject, or an action verb? Write one complete answer.

  1. You do not know if a scarf is in the car. Ask whether one is present.
  2. Everyone knows which scarf you mean. Ask if that scarf is in the office.
  3. The inventory says no rings are in the car. Report this.
  4. the mechanics / not busy Report their condition.
  5. Mia / check each car before it leaves the garage Report her routine.
  6. You do not know whether the owner has an appointment. Ask.
  7. Two gloves are on the back seat. Report what is present.
  8. The car is ready. Ask if the known car is ready.
Sample answers

1. Is there a scarf in the car?

2. Is the scarf in the office?

3. There are no rings in the car.

4. The mechanics are not busy.

5. Mia checks each car before it leaves the garage.

6. Does the owner have an appointment?

7. There are two gloves on the back seat.

8. Is the car ready?

Level 7: read a garage message

Complete the five gaps in Mia's message using grammar you have learned. Then answer the questions about the message.

Hi Leo,

Lena has an appointment tomorrow to pick up the Honda. She asks about a scarf and two rings. There (1) ______ no scarf in the car, and there (2) ______ no rings under the seats. (3) ______ a scarf in the office? (4) ______ two rings in the office? The car (5) ______ ready. Please check the office before Lena arrives.

Thanks,
Mia

Sample answers

1. is   2. are

3. Is there   4. Are there   5. is

Choose the meaning

  1. Who has an appointment tomorrow?
    1. Lena
    2. Leo
    3. Mia
  2. What is Lena looking for?
    1. A scarf and rings
    2. A jacket and gloves
    3. A handbag and buttons
  3. What does Mia say about the car?
    1. It is ready
    2. It is broken
    3. It is missing
  4. Where should Leo look?
    1. In the car
    2. In the office
    3. On the floor
  5. Why is Lena coming?
    1. To pick up the Honda
    2. To repair the car engine
    3. To wash the black Honda
Sample answers

1. A   2. A

3. A   4. B   5. A

Level 8: update Leo

You work at the garage today; Leo works tomorrow. Send him a 3-5 sentence message. Tell him what is in a customer's car and whether anything the owner asked about is missing. Tell him if the car is ready and if the owner has an appointment. You can make up the details and choose your own words.

Before sending: Can Leo understand what to look for and what to tell the owner?

Use the reference if it helps. The exercises above check the grammar; here, give Leo a useful update.
