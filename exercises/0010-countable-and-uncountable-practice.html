Level 6: make sentences with there

Use each note to make a full sentence with there is/are. Make it a question and add a short answer when asked.

Example: In the office: no scarves. Ask if they are there. Answer no.

Answer: Are there scarves in the office? No, there are not.

  1. In the bag: two / tool. Say what is in the bag.
  2. At the garage: no soap. Say what is missing.
  3. At the garage: three / new / tire. Ask if they are there. Answer yes.
  4. This afternoon: no appointments. Say what is missing.
  5. On the back seat: one / handbag. Ask if it is there. Answer yes.
  6. For a service: no money. Ask if money is available. Answer no.
Sample answers

1. There are two tools in the bag.

3. Are there three new tires at the garage? Yes, there are.

2. There is no soap at the garage.

4. There are no appointments this afternoon.

5. Is there a handbag on the back seat? Yes, there is.

6. Is there money for a service? No, there is not.

The first time the writer changed the oil in his car, he spent a few minutes under the car looking for the oil filter. It was not easy to find. Oil filters can be in different places on different engines. His advice is simple: look at the new filter first. Then look for something similar on the engine.
