The first time the writer changed the oil in his car, he spent a few minutes under the car looking for the oil filter. It was not easy to find. Oil filters can be in different places on different engines. His advice is simple: look at the new filter first. Then look for something similar on the engine.

Adapted from the personal experience and advice in the Remove the oil filter section of How to Change Your Motor Oil by Brett & Kate McKay, published October 29, 2009, updated September 26, 2021. The suggested comparison is advice, not another event in the account.
