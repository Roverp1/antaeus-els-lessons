Practice sheet · Lesson 6

Questions with action verbs

Упражнения: вопросы с глаголами действия

Start at Level 1. Each level removes some support.

Начните с уровня 1. Каждый следующий уровень сложнее.

Level 1: prepare the subject

Write he, it, or they.

  1. your brother → ______
  2. the car → ______
  3. your parents → ______
  4. the tires → ______
  5. the engine → ______
  6. the engineers → ______
Answers

1. he   2. it   3. they   4. they   5. it   6. they

Level 2: choose do or does

  1. (Do / Does) you drive to work?
  2. (Do / Does) your brother work at home?
  3. (Do / Does) your parents use an electric car?
  4. (Do / Does) the engine work?
  5. (Do / Does) they practice math every day?
  6. (Do / Does) the car need a new tire?
  7. (Do / Does) we start work at six?
  8. (Do / Does) your brother drive to work?
Answers

1. Do   2. Does   3. Do   4. Does

5. Do   6. Does   7. Do   8. Does

Level 3: choose the basic verb

  1. Does your brother (drive / drives) to work?
  2. Does the car (need / needs) a new tire?
  3. Does the engine (work / works)?
  4. Does your brother (start / starts) work at six?
  5. Do your parents (use / uses) an electric car?
  6. Do they (practice / practices) math every day?

1. drive   2. need   3. work   4. start   5. use   6. practice

Level 7: choose the question type

Choose the correct first word.

  1. (Is / Does) your car new?
  2. (Is / Does) your car need a tire?
  3. (Are / Do) your parents mechanics?
  4. (Are / Do) your parents drive to work?
  5. (Is / Does) the engine powerful?
  6. (Is / Does) the engine work?
  7. (Is / Does) your brother an engineer?
  8. (Is / Does) your brother work at home?

Build the complete question

  1. your car / comfortable?
  2. your car / need a new tire?
  3. your parents / engineers?
  4. your parents / work at home?
  5. the engine / powerful?
  6. the engine / work?
Answers

Choose: 1. Is   2. Does   3. Are   4. Do

5. Is   6. Does   7. Is   8. Does

Level 8: complete sentence families

Write a positive sentence, a negative sentence, and a question for each prompt.

  1. my brother / drive to work
  2. your parents / work at home
  3. the car / need a new tire
  4. the engine / work
Model answers

My brother drives to work.
My brother does not drive to work.
Does my brother drive to work?

Your parents work at home.
Your parents do not work at home.
Do your parents work at home?

The car needs a new tire.

Does the car need a new tire?

The engine works.

Does the engine work?

Level 9: read and complete the dialogue

Use do, does, drive, work, need.

A: ______ you ______ to work?

B: No. I ______ at home.

A: ______ your brother ______ to work?

B: Yes. He drives to work every morning.

A: ______ your brother's car ______ a new tire?

B: No. It does not need a new tire.

Answers

Do you drive to work?

No. I work at home.

Does your brother drive to work?

Does your brother's car need a new tire?

Write
