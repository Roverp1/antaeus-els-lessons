Practice sheet · Lessons 3–5

Present simple practice

Практика: to be и Present Simple

Start at Level 1. Each level removes some support.

Начните с уровня 1. Каждый следующий уровень сложнее.

Level 1: replace the subject

Write he, it, or they.

  1. my brother → ______
  2. the engine → ______
  3. your parents → ______
  4. the tires → ______
  5. the sports car → ______
  6. the engineers → ______
Answers

1. he   2. it   3. they   4. they   5. it   6. they

Level 2: use to be

Choose the correct form

  1. I (am / is / are) a driver.
  2. My brother (am / is / are) an engineer.
  3. The tires (am / is / are) new.
  4. The engine (am / is / are) powerful.
  5. Your parents (am / is / are) at home.
  6. Math (am / is / are) hard.

Make the sentence negative

  1. I am a mechanic.
  2. My brother is a driver.
  3. The car is new.
  4. The tires are cheap.
Answers

I am not a mechanic. / My brother is not a driver.

The car is not new. / The tires are not cheap.

Level 3: positive action verbs

Choose the correct verb

  1. I (drive / drives) to work every morning.
  2. My brother (work / works) at home.
  3. The car (need / needs) a new tire.
  4. Your parents (drive / drives) to work.
  5. The engine (work / works).
  6. We (practice / practices) math every day.

Complete the sentence

Use drive, start, check, use.

  1. I ______ the answer.
  2. My brother ______ work at six.
  3. They ______ an electric car.
  4. My brother ______ to work every morning.
Answers

Choose: 1. drive   2. works   3. needs   4. drive   5. works   6. practice

Complete: 1. check   2. starts   3. use   4. drives

Level 4: negative action verbs

Choose do not or does not

  1. I (do not / does not) drive to work.
  2. My brother (do not / does not) work at home.
  3. Your parents (do not / does not) use an electric car.
  4. The engine (do not / does not) work.
  5. We (do not / does not) practice math every day.
  6. The car (do not / does not) need a new tire.

Make the sentence negative

  1. I drive to work every morning.
  2. My brother starts work at six.
  3. The engine works.
  4. Your parents use an electric car.
Answers

Choose: 1. do not   2. does not   3. do not   4. does not   5. do not   6. does not

I do not drive to work every morning.

My brother does not start work at six.

The engine does not work.

Your parents do not use an electric car.

Level 5: mix all the rules

Choose the correct form

  1. My car (is not / does not) new.
  2. My car (is not / does not) need a new tire.
  3. Your parents (are not / do not) mechanics.
  4. Your parents (are not / do not) drive to work.
  5. The engine (is / works) powerful.
  6. The engine (is / works).
  7. My brother (is not / does not) an engineer.
  8. My brother (is not / does not) work at home.

Repair one error

  1. My brother do not drive to work.
  2. The tires is not new.
  3. The engine does not works.
  4. Your parents does not work at home.
  5. My car does not expensive.
  6. I am not drive to work.

Choose: 1. is not   2. does not   3. are not   4. do not

5. is   6. works   7. is not   8. does not

My brother does not drive to work. / The tires are not new.

Level 6: new words in context

Read these words before the text:

WordMeaningExample
usuallyобычноI usually drive to work.
weekendвыходныеI do not work on the weekend.

washмытьI wash the car on the weekend.

Read

My brother is an engineer. He usually works in an office, but he sometimes works at home. He drives to work in an old SUV. The SUV is comfortable and reliable. It does not need a new engine. My brother does not work on the weekend. He usually washes the SUV on Saturday.

True or false?

Correct every false sentence.

  1. My brother is a mechanic.
  2. He usually works in an office.
  3. He does not drive to work.
  4. The SUV is new.
  5. The SUV needs a new engine.
  6. My brother works on the weekend.
  7. He washes the SUV on Saturday.
Answers

1. False: My brother is an engineer.   2. True

3. False: He drives to work.   4. False: The SUV is old.

5. False: The SUV does not need a new engine.

6. False: My brother does not work on the weekend.   7. True

Level 7: speak and write

Speak

Answer each question with one complete, true sentence.

You only need to answer. You do not need to make questions yet.

  1. Who are you?
  2. What are you not?
  3. Where do you usually work?
  4. What do you do every morning?
  5. What do you not do every day?
  6. Is your car new, old, comfortable, or reliable?

Write

Write six connected sentences about yourself, your work, or your car.

  1. Use to be in one positive sentence.
  2. Use to be in one negative sentence.
  3. Use an action verb in two positive sentences.
  4. Use an action verb in two negative sentences.
  5. Use usually or sometimes at least once.

Model:

I am a driver. I am not a mechanic. I usually drive to work. I sometimes work at home. I do not work on the weekend. My car does not need a new engine.
