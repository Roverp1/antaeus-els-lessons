Homework · Lesson 15

Many and a lot of

Work on the three sessions on different days. Keep the references available.

After checking a sample answer, mark + if your first answer matched or - if you corrected it. Leave the mark empty when there is no visible sample.

Word help

towel = полотенце; dry the car = вытереть машину насухо; rinse = ополаскивать водой; wet = мокрый.

equipment = оборудование; appointment = запись на определённое время; car soap = средство для мытья автомобиля.

Session 1

Level 1: build the noun phrase

Use the expression in the prompt. Make the noun plural where needed. Keep the adjective. Write only the noun phrase.

Example: many / car → many cars.

Example: a lot of / oil → a lot of oil.

Запишите только noun phrase с указанным выражением.

  1. many / battery
  2. a lot of / equipment
  3. many / customer
  4. a lot of / water
  5. a lot of / new / tool
  6. many / appointment
Sample answers

1. many batteries   2. a lot of equipment

3. many customers   4. a lot of water   5. a lot of new tools   6. many appointments

Level 2: choose is or are

Write only is or are. Example: There ______ a lot of oil. → is.

Смотрите на noun после many или a lot of.

  1. There ______ a lot of equipment at this garage.
  2. ______ there many appointments this afternoon?
  3. There ______ not a lot of money for new tires.
  4. There ______ a lot of towels in this box.
  5. ______ there a lot of soap in the bottle?
  6. There ______ not many mechanics at this garage.
Sample answers

1. is   4. are

2. Are   3. is   5. Is   6. are

Level 3: build full sentences with there

Write one complete sentence. Use the expression in the prompt.

Example: equipment / in this garage; positive statement with a lot of → There is a lot of equipment in this garage.

Выберите is/are и нужную noun form. Затем постройте полное предложение.

  1. towel / in the box Make a positive statement with a lot of.
  2. water / in the bucket Make a positive statement with a lot of.
  3. appointment / this morning Make a question with many.
  4. mechanic / at this garage Make a negative statement with many.
  5. money / for new tools Make a question with a lot of. Answer no.
Sample answers

1. There are a lot of towels in the box.

5. Is there a lot of money for new tools? No, there is not.

2. There is a lot of water in the bucket.

3. Are there many appointments this morning?

4. There are not many mechanics at this garage.

Session 2

Level 4: use familiar action verbs

Do this level after studying the action-verb examples in Lesson 15, Section 5. Write one complete sentence for each prompt.

Example: the driver / have / many tools; question → Does the driver have many tools?

После do/does используйте basic verb. Many или a lot of уже указано в задании.

  1. the owner / have / a lot of equipment Make a positive statement.
  2. Mia / have / many customers Make a question.
  3. the mechanics / not need / many new tools Make a negative statement.
  4. this car / use / a lot of gasoline Make a question.
  5. Leo / not have / a lot of money Make a negative statement.
Sample answers

1. The owner has a lot of equipment.

4. Does this car use a lot of gasoline?

2. Does Mia have many customers?

3. The mechanics do not need many new tools.

5. Leo does not have a lot of money.

Level 5: what does the sentence tell you?

Choose the meaning the sentence states: A. None at all. / B. Not a lot.

A: совсем нет. B: количество небольшое, точное число не названо. Выберите то, что предложение сообщает прямо.

Example: There are not any tools. → A.

  1. There are not many appointments this afternoon.
  2. There are not any sponges in the box.
  3. Leo does not have a lot of money.
Sample answers

1. B

2. A   3. B

Not many and not a lot of do not explicitly state zero. They are compatible with zero, so avoid claiming they always mean some are present.

Level 6: before you wash

Read the advice. Write A, B, or C for each question.

Reading help

in the shade = в тени; when it is not sunny = когда не солнечно; drying too quickly = слишком быстро высыхать; leaving spots = оставлять пятна; microfiber towels = полотенца из микрофибры.

Park the car in the shade, or wash it when it is not sunny. This helps prevent the water from drying too quickly and leaving spots. You need two buckets, a sponge, car soap, and two microfiber towels.

Adapted from step 1 of Brett & Kate McKay's car-washing guide.

  1. When is this advice useful?
    1. Before you wash a car
    2. Before you repair an engine
    3. Before you drive to work
  2. Why wash in the shade?
    1. To keep the two towels wet
    2. To stop water from drying quickly
    3. To keep the car wheels clean
  3. What do you need two of?
    1. Two sponges and two bottles
    2. Two engines and two wheels
    3. Two buckets and two towels

For question 2, underline the sentence that helped you choose.

Sample answers

1. A

2. B   3. C

Evidence for 2: This helps prevent the water from drying too quickly and leaving spots.

Level 9: send Leo your idea

Leo wants to wash a car with you this weekend. Write him 4-6 sentences. Suggest a real or imagined place and ask something you need to know. Choose your own facts and wording.

Sample answers

Possible opening: Hi Leo,

One fictional example: Hi Leo, My garage is a good place to wash a car. There is water, and I have two buckets. My sponges are new. I also have some car soap. Do you have a towel?

Accept other places, facts, and wording. Check whether Leo can understand the suggestion and the question. Give selective feedback on familiar grammar. The message does not require many, a lot of, or any other target expression.
