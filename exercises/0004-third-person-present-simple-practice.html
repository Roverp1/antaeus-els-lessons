Choose the word

Use garage, customer, careful.

  1. A mechanic works in a ______.
  2. A person who pays for a service is a ______.
  3. A good mechanic is ______ when he checks a car.
Answers

1. garage   2. customer   3. careful

Level 3: write the correct form

  1. work → ______

Level 8: read and understand

Maria works in a small office near a garage. She usually drives to work. Her car is old, but it is reliable. On Friday, Maria finishes work early. She drives to the garage because the car needs oil. The mechanic checks the engine and fixes a small problem. Maria does not need a new car.

  1. Maria works at home. True or false?
  2. Her car is new. True or false?
  3. Her car needs oil. True or false?
  4. The mechanic checks the engine. True or false?
  5. Maria needs a new car. True or false?
Answers

1. False   2. False   3. True   4. True   5. False

Level 9: write

Write five connected sentences about one person you know.

  1. Say who the person is.
  2. Say where the person works or studies.
  3. Say what the person usually does.
  4. Say what the person does on the weekend.
  5. Write one true negative sentence about the person.

Use at least one verb with -s, one with -es, and one with -ies if you can do so naturally. Do not force a sentence that is not true.
