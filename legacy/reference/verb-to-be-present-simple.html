to be — Present Simple

Reference sheet. Keep nearby when writing English sentences.

Subject pronouns — личные местоимения

Subject to be
fullshort
I — я am 'm
You — ты / вы are 're
He — он is 's
She — она is 's
It — это / он, она (неодуш.) is 's
We — мы are 're
They — они are 're

The rule — правило

I  →  am
You / We / They  →  are
He / She / It  →  is

Subject comes first. English never drops the subject — Russian can, English cannot.

He / She / It's not / isn't
We / They're not / aren't

Question — вопросительная форма

verb + subject?
Am I a driver?   Are you a driver?   Is he fast?

Swap order: verb moves first, subject follows.

Short answers — краткие ответы

QuestionYesNo
Am I ...? Yes, I am. No, I 'm not.
Are you ...? Yes, I am. No, I'm not.
Is he/she/it ...? Yes, he/she is. No, he/she isn't.
Are we/they ...? Yes, we/they are. No, we/they aren't.
