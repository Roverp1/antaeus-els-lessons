# Lesson 4: multiword subjects need practice

The student uses `to be` reliably when the subject is a pronoun and handled most Lesson 4 exercises without much difficulty. He still needs practice identifying complete multiword subjects such as `your parents` and `the tires`, replacing them with the correct pronoun, and locating the main verb when another familiar word appears later in the sentence.

## Evidence

He attempted `you're parents at home`, treated `work` as the main verb in `I am drive to work every morning`, and could not independently repair the ambiguous prompt `Math study hard`. The latter prompt was poorly designed and should not be used as evidence that `Math study` was misunderstood as a valid subject.

## Implications

Lesson 5 should teach present-simple negatives while repeatedly applying subject identification. Questions should wait. Practice should progress from choices to sentence production, then include short reading, speaking, and in-class writing.
