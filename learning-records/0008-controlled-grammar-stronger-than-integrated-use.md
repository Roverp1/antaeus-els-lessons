# Controlled grammar is stronger than integrated use

Lesson 8 and its homework showed that the student can usually identify sentence type and choose `to be` or an action verb when prompted. He performs strongly in staged exercises but can lose those decisions while selecting the complete formula in less-scaffolded reading and personal production. Because he also loses handwritten notes and consults grammar on screen, the next lesson should teach reliable use of a concise digital sentence-builder table before adding another grammar system.

The isolated `customer` singular typo is not evidence of a systematic number problem. Original answers for several corrected reading items are unavailable, so their cause remains unknown.
