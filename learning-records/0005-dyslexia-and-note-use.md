# Dyslexia and note use

The student reports dyslexia and says he has difficulty consulting his notes, so he tries to memorize grammar immediately. Handwriting remains useful during instruction, but the notes must be concise, consistently structured, and built one understood row at a time.

## Implications

Use a complete map plus small rule cards. After guided practice, hide the current card and have him reconstruct only that row from memory. Do not require long copied explanations, and do not add TTS or other accessibility features unless the student identifies a barrier they would solve.
