# Noun number is stronger with explicit cues

Lesson 10 began with difficulty choosing the present-simple verb lane and building the complete form. The student became more confident during the lesson and could complete the later tasks reasonably reliably, but cold present-simple retrieval is not yet secure.

The homework showed strong noun-number performance when `one` or a number was stated directly. The student classified all six phrases correctly, repaired all six noun-phrase errors, and produced correct final forms in most integrated sentences. In the vocabulary task, however, `glove` and `button` stayed singular when the surrounding meaning required `gloves` and `buttons`. Because the source of the crossed-out corrections is mixed or unknown, those corrections are not evidence of independent retrieval.

Reading comprehension was 5/5. The natural message communicated its purpose and included the correct phrase `my black wallet`; `Do you can check it?` used untaught modal grammar and should receive a concise natural recast rather than count as a present-simple target error. The next lesson may introduce positive `there is / there are` only after separate delayed gates for present-simple construction and noun number.
