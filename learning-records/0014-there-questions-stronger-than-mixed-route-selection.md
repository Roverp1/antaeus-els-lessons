Lesson 12 had the right challenge level, with the tutor reporting roughly 90% first-attempt success once the student understood the assignments. Final homework answers show strong control of negative `there` statements, questions, short answers, and repairs; occasional verb-versus-adjective/noun confusion and choosing the sentence's communicative job still need practice.

The source of corrections and reference use are unknown, so these final answers do not establish first-attempt homework accuracy or independent delayed retrieval. ## Implications

Begin Lesson 13 with a brief delayed check, then teach countability and a small optional plural-spelling extension with the reference available. Demonstrate unfamiliar task formats and separate meaning, word role, and construction. Keep the current pace; improve reading quality through the tutor-approved source-subagent and humanizer workflow rather than forcing more target grammar into the text.
