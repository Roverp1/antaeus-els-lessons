# Lesson 5: integration caused global confusion

The student applied complete-subject identification and present-simple action negatives successfully during focused practice. After `to be` and action-verb negatives were mixed, his performance collapsed and he temporarily could not apply even the earlier rules that had been easy at the start.

## Evidence

During the collapse he could no longer identify the complete subject or replace it with a pronoun. Lesson 5 was completed, including its reading section. Cumulative practice Levels 1-4 were assigned as homework, but completion and accuracy have not been verified.

## Implications

The next lesson must begin with delayed retrieval to distinguish temporary fatigue from weak storage. Present-simple action questions may be introduced only after a short mixed-negative gate. Integrated work should separate meaning, grammar-lane selection, subject agreement, and sentence construction before asking for a complete response.
