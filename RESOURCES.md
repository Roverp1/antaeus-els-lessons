# ESL Teaching Resources

Anchored on the Oxford English File Elementary syllabus. Knowledge for
lessons and the grammar spine is drawn from here.

## Knowledge

- [Book: _English File Elementary_ (4th ed.) — Oxford University Press](https://elt.oup.com/catalogue/items/global/teenagers/english_file/9780194031153)
  The syllabus spine. File 1 covers "to be" (am/is/are), subject pronouns,
  greetings, numbers. Use as the canonical grammar sequence. The student
  should follow this order: to be -> present simple -> there is/are ->
  plurals/quantifiers.
- [Book: _Cambridge Grammar of English_ — Carter & McCarthy](https://www.cambridge.org/gb/english/catalog/grammar-vocabulary-and-pronunciation/cambridge-grammar-english/)
  Reference for tricky grammar decisions when a rule needs verifying. Not a
  teaching text; reach for it when English File's explanation is too thin.
- [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/)
  Use for every new word introduced. Has level (A1-C2) tags, Russian
  translations via the Russian-English section, and IPA pronunciation. Use
  before introducing any vocab item to confirm its CEFR level.
- [YouTube: Learn English with Rebecca — "to be" lessons](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=learn+english+to+be+verb)
  Short, free explainers to assign as supplementary viewing. Pick one that
  matches the lesson content and link it in the lesson's "primary source"
  section. Avoid long lecture-style ones; aim for under 10 minutes.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/EnglishLearning](https://www.reddit.com/r/EnglishLearning/)
  Community where the student could (later) post and get corrections from
  native speakers and other learners. Begin with reading, not posting, until
  his confidence in sentence construction is solid.

## Gaps

- No paid English File student book yet in the repo; rely on syllabus
  summaries and free online grammar references until one is sourced.
- No community specifically for Russian-L1 English learners noted; consider
  adding one if the student wants peer interaction.
