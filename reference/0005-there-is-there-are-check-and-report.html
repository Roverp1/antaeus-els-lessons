Is there? Are there?

Что есть? Чего нет?

Look at the noun: singular or plural? Keep this sheet open while practising.

Смотрите на существительное: единственное число или множественное?

One thing or several?

JobOne: scarfPlural: rings
Present There is a scarf in the car. There are two rings in the car.
Absent There is no scarf in the car. There are no rings in the car.
Ask Is there a scarf in the car? Are there two rings in the car?

No + noun means zero of that thing. Do not put a/an after no.

No scarf is singular → there is. No rings is plural → there are.

Даже когда предметов нет, форму is/are выбираем по слову scarf/rings.

Answer the question

Question beginsYesNo
Is there a scarf...?Yes, there is.No, there is not.
Are there two rings...?Yes, there are.No, there are not.
