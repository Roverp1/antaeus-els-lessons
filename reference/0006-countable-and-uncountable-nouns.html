Countable: car, engine, glove, ring, appointment. Uncountable here: oil as a liquid for an engine, water as a liquid, money as an amount.

Количество воды можно измерить, но само слово water здесь не получает число или окончание -s. Money тоже uncountable в English: The money is..., хотя по-русски «деньги».

Use the familiar forms

JobExample
Report what is presentThere is oil on the floor.
Report what is absentThere is no oil on the floor.
Ask if it is presentIs there oil on the floor?
AnswerYes, there is. / No, there is not.

With no, use the noun form shown: no glove → is, no gloves → are, no oil → is. Do not add a/an after no.

Known items can still use the: The gloves are in the office. The water is on the floor.

The owner needs money for a new engine.

Does the owner need money for a new engine?

Need is a verb. The noun money does not change the verb route.
