Present simple: positive

Present Simple: утвердительные предложения

Use present simple for routines, habits, facts, and schedules.

Рутина, привычки, факты, расписания.

The form

Subject Verb Example
I / You / We / TheydriveThey drive to work.
He / She / ItdrivesShe drives a Subaru.
one person or thingdrivesMy brother drives a Subaru.

Rule

He / She / It / one person or thing + verb-s

I work.   My brother works.   The engine needs oil.

Third-person spelling

Verb ending Change Examples
most verbs add -s work → works
need → needs
-ch, -ss, -sh, -x, -zz add -es wash → washes
fix → fixes
consonant + y change y to i, add -es study → studies
useful forms to remember learn the complete form have → has
go → goes
do → does

The ending moves to does in negatives and questions.

He washes his car.

He does not wash his car.

Does he wash his car?

When to use it

UseExample
routineI drive to work every day.
habitHe drinks coffee in the morning.
factA car needs fuel.
scheduleThe class starts at six.

To be or an action verb

My car is fast. To be: a description.

My brother drives a Subaru. Action verb: drive.

My brother is drives a Subaru. Wrong.
