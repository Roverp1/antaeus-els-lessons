There is or there are?

Что находится в определённом месте?

Choose the number, then match the opening and noun.

Определите количество, затем согласуйте начало предложения и существительное.

Follow four steps

  1. Ask: one or more than one?
  2. Choose there is or there are.
  3. Build the matching singular or plural noun phrase.
  4. Add the place.
Meaning Opening Noun phrase Complete example
one There is a/an + singular noun There is an earring under the seat.
more than one There are number + plural noun There are two gloves on the floor.

Number controls two places.

one → is + singular noun

more than one → are + plural noun

Choose by the thing you report

The location does not control is or are.

There are two gloves in the car.

two gloves → more than one → there are

The singular word car is only part of the location.

Check both signals

CorrectIncorrect
There is a jacket on the seat. There are a jacket on the seat.
There are three buttons in the office. There is three button in the office.
