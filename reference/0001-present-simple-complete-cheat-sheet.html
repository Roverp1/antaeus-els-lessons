Present simple: complete map

Present Simple: полная схема

First choose: to be or an action verb.

Сначала выберите: to be или глагол действия.

How to build any present-simple sentence you know

Make one decision at a time. Do not try to build the whole sentence immediately.

Sentence algorithm

  1. Decide the meaning. Is it a positive sentence, a negative sentence, or a question?
  2. Find the complete subject. Who or what is the sentence about? Keep all the subject words together.
  3. Replace the subject with a pronoun. Use I, you, he, she, it, we, or they. This tells you which grammar form to choose.
  4. Choose the verb type. Does the intended sentence already contain an action verb such as work, drive, need, wash, study, or have?
  5. Choose the subject group. Is it I, one person or thing, or you/we/they and more than one?
  6. Choose the sentence form. Use the positive, negative, or question row for that verb type and subject group.
  7. Check the complete sentence. Does it have an explicit subject and exactly one main verb pattern?

Step 1: decide the meaning

MeaningFormExample
Give informationpositiveThe car is reliable.
Say something is not truenegativeThe car is not reliable.
Ask if something is truequestionIs the car reliable?

Step 2: find the complete subject

[Your parents] work at home.

[The car owner] washes the car.

[My brother's car] is reliable.

Не выбирайте только первое слово. Всё название человека или предмета является подлежащим.

Step 3: replace the complete subject

your parents→they
the car owner→he / she
my brother's car→it

Step 4: choose to be or an action verb

QuestionDecisionExample
Is there an action verb: work, need, have, drive, wash...? Yes → action verb The car needs oil.
Is the rest an identity, description, or place? Yes → to be The car is reliable.

Important: an “action verb” in this course does not always mean physical movement. Need and have use the action-verb grammar pattern too.

Step 7: final check

  1. Did I write the complete subject?
  2. Did I choose to be or an action verb?
  3. Does the verb agree with the complete subject?
  4. After do/does, did I use the basic verb?
  5. Did I avoid putting am/is/are before an action verb?

Two complete examples

Prompt: your brother / not available

Meaning: negative → Subject: your brother → Pronoun: he → No action verb → to be → one person → Your brother is not available.

Prompt: your brother / not work today

Meaning: negative → Subject: your brother → Pronoun: he → Action verb: work → one person → Your brother does not work today.
