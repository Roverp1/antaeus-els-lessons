Quick reference · Lesson 15

Many and a lot of

Many + plural countable noun.
A lot of + plural countable or uncountable noun.

Много предметов или большое количество.
Noun по-прежнему определяет is/are: tools → are; equipment → is.

Three sentence jobs

JobPlural countable nounUncountable noun
Positive statementThere are a lot of tools.There is a lot of equipment.
QuestionAre there many tools?Is there a lot of equipment?
Negative statementThere are not many tools.There is not a lot of equipment.

Keep the noun form

Keep all three words in a lot of. Use many directly before these nouns: many tools.

Common use

In everyday positive statements, use a lot of as a default. Many is common in questions and negatives.

Both work with plural nouns: Are there many tools? / Are there a lot of tools? Many is also possible in positive statements.

Quantity and absence

ExpressionMeaning
some toolsno exact number
a lot of toolsa large number
not many toolsnot a large number
not any toolsnone

Some does not necessarily mean a small number.

Not many не сообщает точное число. Not any прямо сообщает: совсем нет.

Not a lot of water says the amount is not large. It does not explicitly say there is no water.

With have, need, and use

The owner has a lot of tools. → Does the owner have many tools? → The owner does not have many tools.

Does the garage use a lot of water? / Mia does not have a lot of money.
