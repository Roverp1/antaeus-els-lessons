3. Present simple with to be

Subject Positive Negative Question
I I am available. I am not busy. Am I available?
he / she / it
one person or thing		 The mechanic is careful. The mechanic is not busy. Is the mechanic careful?
you / we / they
more than one		 The tires are new. The tires are not old. Are the tires new?

To make a question, move am/is/are before the subject.

The car is reliable. → Is the car reliable?

Short answers with to be

QuestionYesNo
Is he/she/it...?Yes, he/she/it is.No, he/she/it is not.
Are you/we/they...?Yes, you/we/they are.No, you/we/they are not.

4. Present simple with action verbs

Subject Positive Negative Question
I / you / we / they
more than one		 They work here. They do not work here. Do they work here?
he / she / it
one person or thing		 He works here. He does not work here. Does he work here?

Use the basic verb after do not, does not, do, and does.

He works. → He does not work. → Does he work?

He does not works.   Does he works?

Short answers with action verbs

QuestionYesNo
Does he/she/it...?Yes, he/she/it does.No, he/she/it does not.
Do I/you/we/they...?Yes, I/you/we/they do.No, I/you/we/they do not.

6. Complete sentence families

Type Positive Negative Question + answer
to be The car is reliable. The car is not reliable. Is the car reliable?
Yes, it is.
action verb The car needs oil. The car does not need oil. Does the car need oil?
Yes, it does.
to be Your parents are busy. Your parents are not busy. Are your parents busy?
No, they are not.
action verb Your parents work at home. Your parents do not work at home. Do your parents work at home?
No, they do not.
